Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

This website is a personal portfolio belonging to Alvin Kiiru. I take your privacy seriously and am committed to being transparent about how I handle any information you share with me.

Information I Collect

This site does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. The only information I may receive is what you voluntarily provide by submitting the contact form (your name, email address, and message). That data is collected via Google Forms and stored in my Google account.

How I Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry or message.
  • To follow up on any collaboration or opportunity you mention.
  • I will never sell, rent, or share your personal details with third parties for marketing.

Third-Party Services

This site uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts — for typography.
  • Google Forms / Google Maps — for the contact form and map embed.
  • Google Sheets + Apps Script — to power the Insights blog feed.
  • Unsplash — for placeholder imagery.

I encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle data.

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies. Third-party embeds (Google Maps, Forms) may set cookies governed by their respective policies.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of any personal data you have submitted by contacting me directly through the contact page. I will honour all reasonable requests promptly.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please reach out via the contact form or through any of the social links in the footer.
